# Schoolhouse · primary and secondary schools: design notes

Batch D (SoraLearn education themes), third build, 21 November 2026.

- Character: warm and orderly. School navy with a burnt-orange accent (white text on it passes WCAG AA), rounded headings, pill buttons, generous spacing: friendly for families, still professional.
- Sections: At a glance; Term dates and events (a dated list, the most-used part of a school site); School news; Learning at our school (learning areas or year groups); Enrolling your child (numbered steps); Our leadership team; Come for a tour.
- Families read school sites on phones: term dates stay a single readable column at 390 px, and the tour button stays near the top.
- Rendered by the batch C section renderer in SoraSpace, with its own colours, type and section list.
- No Student sign-in link (children's accounts wait on their own plan). Photographs of children need parents' written consent.
- Still to do to reach the Explore benchmark: stock photography with credits, and reviewed desktop and phone screens.
