# SoraTrip standalone sales page and mockup scope

SoraTrip is a separate planned app, sold independently or as a selectable SoraSpace service/bundle. Shared sign-in, storage and billing remain platform foundations; Journal and Photography are optional connections rather than prerequisites. No price or release date is approved.

Public route: `/tools/soratrip/`, with a homepage navigation link, a prominent travel section and an app-directory entry on both staging and SoraSpace public marketing builds. The existing Journeys & activities entry remains separately listed so map/fitness work is not lost or relabelled as SoraTrip. The directory now contains 32 app/module entries.

Five detailed, static mockups are travel-specific:

1. Compact trip list, day-by-day itinerary and selected-item inspector; flights, stays, activities and rail, with explicit local dates/timezones.
2. Departure/arrival item editor with IANA zones, UTC offsets, supplier/reference and private notes.
3. Private document list, selected offline files and a fictional travel-document/QR placeholder.
4. Phone offline-reader and interrupted-update states, last-saved/readiness information, selected files, remove/update and PDF-backup concepts.
5. Optional linked Journal entries and Photography gallery, preserving standalone travel planning and separate publishing boundaries.

The page includes nine balanced benefit cards, manual/import/provider distinctions, privacy and sharing choices, standalone versus SoraSpace packaging and practical FAQs. Mock controls are inert text/spans; there is no purchase, booking, live alert, participant invitation, document upload or offline download action. Every figure is labelled an illustrative design concept using fictional data. No real customer, booking, carrier feed, passport, QR code or private app screenshot is used.

The overview/headline, navigation and metadata have 16-language catalogue coverage and correct locale/canonical/hreflang links. Detailed design descriptions and fictional mockup content are explicitly labelled English; full editorial translation is a remaining marketing task. Source captions do not claim a working offline reader or released SoraTrip planner. Local tests exercise 320/390/768/1366px layouts, five distinct mockups, route/anchor links, metadata/sitemap coverage and absence of live app/network actions.
