# SoraSpace sales feature sheet

**Availability:** In testing means a restricted pilot/preview. In development and Planned describe future capabilities, not included access today. No approved prices, release dates or universal format/provider compatibility are promised.

## Feature areas

### My writing & Journal — In testing

- Journals, essays, articles and reports with your own writing types
- Familiar formatting, regional spelling, tables, links and inserted images
- Autosave, recovery, Save As and private/public reviewed publication
- Image resizing, cropping, adjustments, covers and galleries
- Social sharing and a developing Save to SoraResearch inbox

### Translation beside your original — Planned

- Translate to a selected language in My writing and Journal
- Keep the original on the left and an editable translation on the right
- Linked saved language versions, with a review flag when the source changes
- Publish the language version you choose; preserve images, structure and citations
- Use on-device processing where supported; explain model downloads and resource requirements

### SoraResearch — Planned

- Compact resizable three-pane library with dense reference rows, tabs and full item metadata
- Nested collections, multiple collection membership, coloured tags and live saved searches
- Duplicate/unfiled/retracted items, trash and research feeds
- Collect published My writing and Journal posts into your research workspace
- DOI/ISBN capture, duplicate review and BibTeX/RIS import and export
- PDF reading, annotations, notes, related sources and literature-review projects
- CSL styles, editable citations and dynamic bibliographies in SoraDocs; planned Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs interoperability
- One-click browser capture and metadata detection with reviewed Connector support
- A supported Zotero connection, with private and shared libraries and clear sync controls

### SoraShop — Planned

- Your own storefronts, product collections, variants and product photographs
- Physical and digital products, stock, made-to-order dispatch estimates and delivery
- Product costing, order management, discounts and customer communication
- Reviewed checkout, payments, refunds, invoices and fulfilment workflows
- Workspace roles, store permissions and merchant-controlled catalogue imports
- Shop reports and links to the wider SoraSpace business workspace

### Office apps — In development

- SoraDocs document editing, page layout and references
- SoraSheets spreadsheets, formulas and data views
- SoraMail, shared contacts and calendar workflows
- Presentations and export workflows with consistent app controls

### Photography & image creation — In testing

- SoraPhotos photo development and SoraImage image editing previews
- Private galleries, sub-galleries, covers, captions, descriptions and tags
- Small and large thumbnail grids, local search suggestions and date/size/name views
- Gallery organisation, retained image edits and private sharing controls
- Public gallery publishing and featured selections are planned

### Creative, PDF, music & video — In development

- SoraPDF reading, annotation and page organisation previews
- Planned illustration, page layout, interface design and CAD tools
- Planned audio, video and motion workflows
- Consistent file workflows and app-specific format/export qualification

### Your website & pages — Planned

- Personal, portfolio, academic, business and shop page templates
- Guided page creation, editable menus and authorised publishing
- Theme presets, colours, fonts, logos, page blocks and responsive previews
- Your tenant address and verified custom-domain connection

### Work together — Planned

- Individual accounts and workgroups with permission-controlled membership
- Invite and manage colleagues within your subscribed seat allocation
- Granular file access for named people, including read and read/write
- Shared projects, research and content with private personal areas
- Platform support and tenant management with customer boundaries

### Files & storage — In development

- Private files, folders, versions, restore and clear save destinations
- Local-device work alongside hosted storage options
- A shared tenant storage allowance across subscribed apps
- Connected storage choices and transfers after provider qualification
- Understandable usage, limits and recovery controls

### SoraProjects — Planned

- Projects, jobs, tasks, boards and reusable templates
- Assignments, priorities, dates, calendars and dependencies

### SoraCRM & business operations — Planned

- Linked customer and project records with permission-aware access

### Sharing, messaging & support — Planned

- Reviewed social sharing and custom title/summary/link templates
- Supported professional, academic and private-messaging handoffs
- Internal and workgroup conversations with controlled sharing
- Service notices, support requests and consent-based newsletters

### Journeys, maps & activities — Planned

- A trip planner within Journal, with day-by-day flights, stays, transport and activities

- Exercise integrations with explicit provider permission

### Make SoraSpace yours — In development

### Account, subscriptions & protection — Planned

### Optional assistance — Planned

- Opt-in writing and post-summary assistance with editable results
- Hashtag and summary suggestions for reviewed sharing
- Clear processing choices, permissions and usage controls

## Mockup and claims rules

Mockups contain fictional sample references, products and metrics. Static controls are not connected apps or customer data. Translation uses prewritten example text, not a live translation engine. SoraResearch’s full reference library and Zotero sync remain planned; the separate private inbox pilot is not the complete research app. SoraShop checkout/orders remain planned. Existing My Studio captures retain their capture provenance.

Customer-facing task areas are grouped above; internal migrations, founder exemptions, infrastructure investigations and deferred mobile-store packaging are not advertised as available customer products. Current web-only scope remains. New detailed roadmap copy is English; existing locale navigation/canonical routes are retained and further copy localisation remains part of release work.
