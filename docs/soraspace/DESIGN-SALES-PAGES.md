2. Linked story frames plus five reusable typography styles. A right-hand page-3 frame starts at X16/Y32 mm and measures 120 × 150 mm. The 120 mm live width, two columns and 5 mm gutter yield 57.5 mm columns. System-font previews are explicit; the separate cover style illustrates a fictional unavailable display font with substitution.
3. Four sample placed-asset links, including source pixels, placement dimensions and effective resolution. 1440 × 888 px at 120 × 74 mm gives 304.8 ppi; 800 × 600 px at 120 × 90 mm gives 169.3 ppi, below the sample 300 ppi review target. No source image is fetched or replaced.
4. Six preflight findings: three passing checks (page count, bleed, sample links) and three unresolved items (missing fictional cover font, low-resolution page-4 image, overset page-5 text). Output is not represented as ready or certified.
5. Separate planned print/digital PDF reviews. Adding 3 mm on every side makes the 148 × 210 mm trim 154 × 216 mm with bleed. Digital output omits bleed in its sample choice. Neither file is generated; font/colour/links, accessibility, PDF standards and print-provider acceptance need real engine tests.
