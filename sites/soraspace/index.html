Research & project management · In development

For researchers, academics and creative teams.

Zotero integration & research management: Planned research tools bring sources, reading notes, citations, bibliographies and Zotero compatibility into your workspace.

Project management: Planned SoraSpace Projects connects jobs and sub-jobs, time tracking, alterations, hourly or fixed fees, invoices and online payments.

Zotero integration is documented in the desktop Docs engine. Browser integration requires separate work and is not currently offered.
  1. Collect sources — planned reference imports and a Zotero-compatible library.
  2. Read and develop ideas — private notes linked to sources and projects.
  3. Write and reference — preserve citations and verify bibliographies in supported workflows.
  4. Choose what to publish — private research journals remain separate from public articles.

Research Library, Projects and optional AI assistance are planned features. No AI provider receives files through this page.
